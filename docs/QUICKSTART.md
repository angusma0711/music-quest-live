# 老師快速指南 Teacher quick-start

1. **Login 登入**: open `/teacher` on the projector laptop and sign in with your teacher account.
2. **Create room 建立房間**: press **Create new session 建立新房間**.
3. **Show join code 顯示號碼**: tap the code/QR box at the top-left to show a large QR and the code. Pupils scan it, or open `/join` and type the code and a nickname or class number (e.g. `5B-12`). Watch the **joined / online** counters.
4. **Launch question 開始**: press **Start adventure 開始冒險**. Every iPad shows question 1 automatically.
   - **Listening**: press play on the teacher screen. iPads stay silent.
   - **Rhythm**: after the reveal, invite the class to clap.
   - **Reflection 💬**: a discussion prompt with no answers collected.
5. **Pause 暫停 / Resume 繼續**: stops or resumes submissions. A paused question accepts no answers, even from a reloaded iPad.
6. **Close 截止 → Reveal 揭曉**: close answering, then reveal. iPads show the correct answer, the explanation and the pupil's own result. The teacher sees the distribution and the number correct. Tick **show distribution** to see counts before revealing; correctness stays hidden until the reveal.
7. **Next 下一題 / Clear quest 完成關卡**: the big green button always shows the next sensible step. After a reveal or a cleared quest you can also:
   - tap a quest on the map to jump to it, or
   - press **Waiting screen 等候畫面** to pause the game for discussion.
8. **Results 結果**: shows class totals per question. No individual pupil data.
9. **End 結束**: ends the session and cancels the code. **New session 新房間** starts fresh.

## Recovering from Wi-Fi problems 網絡問題
- **Teacher laptop reloads or loses Wi-Fi**: reopen `/teacher`. It returns to the same room and the same question. Pupils just wait.
- **A pupil's iPad drops or Safari reloads**: reopen `/join` on the **same iPad and same browser**. The pupil rejoins automatically with their answer kept, and cannot answer twice.
- **A pupil uses a different iPad**: they join again as a new nickname.
- **"Reconnecting… 重新連線中"** on a screen: wait a few seconds; it re-syncs by itself.
- **The whole network fails**: switch to the paper fallback in [READINESS.md](READINESS.md).
